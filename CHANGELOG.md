# Changelog

Every release of Taller de Despiece, newest first. Planned work lives in the [roadmap](README.md#roadmap).

## [1.0.0] · 2026-10-24

First public release.

### Added
- Catalog of 11 parametric furniture types: bookcase, desk, chest of drawers, nightstand, wardrobe, TV stand, base cabinet, wall cabinet, shoe cabinet, coffee table and bench.
- Tape-measure sliders for width, height and depth, drag handles on the drawing and exact numeric input.
- Shelves, dividers, drawers with slide length picked from the depth, doors, plinth, back panel, desk drawer unit and modesty panel.
- Oblique 3D drawing in SVG with doors that swing open and drawers that slide out, one by one or all at once.
- Cut list with edge banding per side, guillotine cutting plan on 2600 × 1830 mm sheets and a shopping list with hardware.
- Woodworking warnings: sagging shelves, oversized doors, missing back panel or modesty panel, tall units that need wall anchoring.
- Order text ready to paste into WhatsApp or email.
- Light and dark themes, responsive layout, keyboard support and reduced-motion support.
- Deployment on Cloudflare Workers as a static site.
